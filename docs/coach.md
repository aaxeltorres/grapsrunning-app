# src/coach

Coach Mike's scripted conversations and the plan model, pure TypeScript (no React). Moved verbatim from CLAUDE.md.

- `src/coach`: Coach Mike's scripted conversations, pure TypeScript (no React)
  - `planOnboardingScript.ts`: the Plan onboarding script. All of Mike's copy, questions, options and reactions live here.
  - `types.ts`: script step types (message, question, recap).
  - `conversation.ts`: builds the chat transcript from the script plus the saved answers (resume, edits, recap).
  - `runnerProfile.ts`: the `RunnerProfile` model. Stores stable option ids and metric values, never display labels. `planLength` ('weekly' / 'monthly') is a plan answer: read it with `planLengthOf` (missing means monthly; `usePlanOnboarding` records 'monthly' on profiles that finished the onboarding before the question), and changing it adds a line about it to the Your profile sheet (`planLengthChanged`). `planAnswersSnapshot(profile)` is the normalized form of the plan answers (goal, level, speed work, days, plan length, injuries; order-insensitive; age, height and weight only through their effect, `modifiers`, left out when neutral so older snapshots still match) and `samePlanAnswers` compares two. `planModifiers(profile)` turns age, height and weight into a few bands, never shown: `progression` 'steady' (age 14-17 and 40-54) or 'gentle' (55+), and `softStart` for a run/walk beginner with weight / height² of 30 or more; out-of-range answers count as missing. So 25 to 26 changes nothing and crossing a band shows the leave sheet. `intensityBy` is a setting (missing means pace), not a plan answer. `LevelId` has eight levels, the top three being `run_half`, `run_marathon` and `run_competitive` (advanced like `run_10k_plus`, each with its own easy pace in `EASY_PACE`, same volume and rep numbers as `run_10k_plus` for now); read a saved level with `knownLevel`, which returns `undefined` for an id this version doesn't know (treated as no level by `rulesFor` and `planAnswersSnapshot`).
  - `plan.ts`: the `Plan` / `Workout` model (dated workouts made of steps and repeat groups; meters, seconds, seconds per km; a run workout has status `completed` / `partial` and an optional `result`, with optional per-rep `reps`, all additive so old saved plans still load; `Plan.length` is 'weekly' or 'monthly', missing means monthly, and `weeks` counts the weeks generated so far; `basedOn` is the plan answers it was built from and `keptAnswers` the ones the user kept it for, and `planNeedsRebuild(plan, answers, fallback)` is the rule behind the Your profile leave sheet: it shows only when the answers equal neither, with the profile as it was when Your profile opened as the fallback for plans without `basedOn`). Also additive: `type` is the category (easy, aerobic, tempo, long, intervals, speed, rest) and `session` the specific session; a step may have a `zone` (1-5) and a `manual` target (ends on Done / Ready); a repeat group may have `sets`, a `macroRest` and `noFinalRest` (no rest after its very last rep). Step kinds: `recovery` is the micro rest, `macroRest` the rest between sets, `steady` continuous running. Pure helpers: `unrollSegments` (the one unrolling of reps and sets, with indices), `totalDistance`, `totalDuration`, `displayName`, `structureLine`, `topZone`, `isDemandingType`... Plain JSON, so an AI can produce it.
  - `generatePlan.ts`: `generatePlan(profile, today, { weeks })` returns a deterministic rule-based mock plan (four weeks for a monthly plan, one for a weekly plan); the AI integration goes here and in `generateWeek`. `extendPlan` adds the current week to a weekly plan once it starts (by its calendar week index, so the pattern continues; weeks the app was not opened in stay empty; existing workouts are never touched). `generatePlan` and `extendPlan` stamp `Plan.basedOn` (the plan answers the plan was built from; an extension clears `keptAnswers`). `createNewPlan(plan, profile)` is the only way to rebuild a plan (the Your profile row and its leave sheet both call it) and replaces every planned workout from today on (edited too) and keeps the rest, with no duplicate dates. Its rules: no demanding sessions (long, intervals, speed, tempo) on consecutive days, interval-type sessions only with speed work, advanced sessions from the 5K level up (`isAdvancedLevel`, including the half, marathon and competitive levels), beginners and runners in pain keep run/walk / easy plans. Weeks come in blocks of four (build, build, build, lighter): each week's total time follows `WEEK_CURVE` (1, 1.08, 1.16, 0.92 of week 1; +5% per later block, up to +20%), fitted by one load `s` per week (`fitWeek`: session flex parts, easy and regenerative runs, gentle minutes, beginners' run bouts) that keeps build weeks within 0 to +10% of the week before and the fourth week 15-25% below the third, in time and distance; the long run has its own curve (`LONG_CURVE`, shorter in week 4), beginners drop a rep in week 4, and the longest quality session sits in week 3. Sessions and classic intervals take their variant from the week, so a session never repeats with the same structure on the same weekday in consecutive weeks. The 10K level and up (not beginners, not in pain) have their own volume (`LEVEL_VOLUME`, with `GOAL_VOLUME` by goal id; `Rules.volume`): week 1 is fitted to minutes per training day × days × the goal factor (`fitFirstWeek`, capped so the build weeks can still grow), the week-1 long run sits between the level's floor and ceiling by goal (10K level: at least 10 km; capped at 32 km and the editor's 180 min), sessions get `extraReps` and a bigger `SESSION_LEVEL_SCALE` and stay within `SESSION_SPECS`, classic intervals within 75 min and easy runs within 120 min; they fit on a wider load grid (`fitLoads`). Levels below 10K keep the original path, unchanged. From `planModifiers`: `Rules.growth` (1, 0.75 steady, 0.5 gentle) scales the build-up only (build weeks' growth, their weekly cap and the block growth, through `curveAt` / `weekLoad`); week 1 and the lighter week's 15-25% drop stay the same; 'gentle' also turns the second aerobic session into an easy run of the same length (`moreRecovery`, `easyFor`), so the time on feet stays and the intensity drops; `softStart` builds a beginner's week 1 at load 0.9. The day after a demanding one is regenerative, but not at the start of a plan: when that day before is not in the plan (Monday of week 1, or a past day dropped from a plan created mid-week) it is a plain easy run (`weekWorkouts`; `extendPlan` passes the plan's own workouts). A block builds base volume only: no peak, taper, race date or race-pace work.
  - `sessions.ts`: the templates of each structured session in variants (reps, rep length or zone split; `SESSION_SPECS`: category, speed work, advanced, editor limits) and `buildSession`, which scales the continuous parts to a total time and leaves rep blocks fixed; `variant` (the plan week) picks the variant, and `extraReps` adds reps to each rep block for the strong levels (`sessionExtraReps`, passed by the generator and the editor alike). Shared by the generator and the editor, which rebuilds a session with its week's variant.
  - `zones.ts`: zones Z1-Z5 (name, feel) and their pace ranges from the easy pace, clamped to the app's pace limits. By pace only: no heart rate yet.
  - `workoutEditor.ts`: pure logic of the workout editor (run types including every session, limits, warm-up / main / cool-down layout, the workout built from a draft, the hard-session warning; `layoutTable` builds every amount's layout once per run type, so the duration bar's drag never rebuilds a session). Built on the generator's rules and paces; sessions are edited by total minutes.
  - `planOnboardingScript.ts` also asks the plan length (after the training days).
  - Mike's Plan screen one-liners (`planDayMessages`) and his workout editor lines (`editorMessages`) also live in `planOnboardingScript.ts`.
  - Scripted chats are not connected to the AI chatbot, and every answer is a predefined option (no free text).
